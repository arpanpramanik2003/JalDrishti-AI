PACKAGE OF PRACTICES: MAIZE / CORN (INDIA)

1. WATER & IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT:
- Maize is extremely sensitive to both drought and waterlogging. Ensure effective field surface drainage.
- Critical Irrigation Stages: Tasseling stage (45-50 days after sowing) and Silking / Grain filling stage (60-70 days after sowing).
- Water stress at tasseling causes poor pollination and barren cobs.

2. PEST & DISEASE CONTROL:
- Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda): Devastating invasive caterpillar feeding inside central leaf funnels creating ragged holes and heavy sawdust-like poop (frass).
  * Chemical Control: Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L water (80 g/acre) OR Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 mL/L water (100 mL/acre) directed into plant whorls.
  * Organic/Bio Treatment: Apply entomopathogenic fungus Metarhizium anisopliae @ 5 g/L OR drop sand + neem cake mixture (9:1 ratio) directly into central plant whorls.
  * Preventive Cultural Tip: Deep summer plowing to expose overwintering pupae to predatory birds.

3. FERTILIZER APPLICATION:
- Recommended NPK Ratio: 60:30:30 kg/acre.
- Apply full Phosphorus, Potassium, and 25% Nitrogen as basal at sowing.
- Top dress 50% Nitrogen at knee-high stage (30 DAS) and remaining 25% Nitrogen at tasseling stage (50 DAS).
