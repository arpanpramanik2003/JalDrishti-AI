PACKAGE OF PRACTICES: PADDY RICE (WEST BENGAL)

1. WATER MANAGEMENT:
- Paddy rice requires standing water of 2-5 cm during the tillering and flowering stages.
- Drain the field completely 10-15 days before harvesting.
- In case of water scarcity, adopt Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) to save up to 30% water.

2. PEST & DISEASE CONTROL:
- Yellow Stem Borer: Symptoms include deadhearts in early stages and whiteheads during flowering. Treatment: Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10 kg/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre.
- Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): Causes hopper burn (drying of leaves). Treatment: Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 50 ml/acre or Pymetrozine 50% WG @ 120 g/acre. Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer.
- Blast Disease: Spindle-shaped spots on leaves. Treatment: Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 120 g/acre.

3. FERTILIZER APPLICATION:
- Recommended NPK ratio: 60:30:30 kg/acre for high-yielding varieties.
- Apply full dose of Phosphorus and Potash at transplanting. Apply Nitrogen in 3 split doses (Transplanting, Active Tillering, Panicle Initiation).
