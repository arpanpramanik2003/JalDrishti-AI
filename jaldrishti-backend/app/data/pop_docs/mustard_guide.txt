PACKAGE OF PRACTICES: MUSTARD / RAPESEED (INDIA)

1. WATER & IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT:
- Mustard requires 2-3 irrigations during the crop lifecycle.
- First critical irrigation: Branching stage (25-30 days after sowing).
- Second critical irrigation: Pod formation / Siliquae development stage (50-60 days after sowing).
- Avoid heavy flooding during flowering to prevent lodging and aphid outbreak.

2. PEST & DISEASE CONTROL:
- Mustard Aphid (Lipaphis erysimi): Small green/black sucking insects forming dense colonies on inflorescence, pods, and leaves causing wilting and sap depletion.
  * Chemical Spray: Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 80 g/acre OR Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L water (350 mL/acre).
  * Organic/Bio Treatment: Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) @ 50 mL/L OR Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm @ 2 mL/L.
  * Cultural Escape Tip: Early sowing before October 25 significantly escapes severe aphid infestation.
- Alternaria Leaf Blight & White Rust: Concentric dark spots on leaves and white pustules on flower heads.
  * Chemical Control: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L OR Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L.

3. FERTILIZER APPLICATION:
- Recommended NPK & Sulfur: 32:16:16 kg/acre + 10 kg Elemental Sulfur per acre.
- Apply full Phosphorus, Potassium, Sulfur, and 50% Nitrogen as basal application at sowing.
- Top dress remaining 50% Nitrogen after the first irrigation at 30 days after sowing.
